Part I. Tóm tắt kiến thức

1. Unless = If … not (Nếu … không / Trừ khi)

Unless + S + V (khẳng định), S + will + V

I will not be happy unless you play games with me.
✘ Unless I don’t play games… → sau unless không dùng phủ định.

2. Although / Despite / In spite of (Mặc dù)

Although + S + V, S + V
Despite / In spite of + V-ing, S + V (cùng chủ ngữ)
Despite / In spite of + Noun, S + V

Although he is rich, he doesn’t have a girlfriend.
Despite being rich, he doesn’t have a girlfriend.
Despite the bad weather, we went out.

3. Because / Because of (Bởi vì)

Because + S + V   Because of + V-ing / Noun

Because he was ill, he was absent today.
Because of being ill / his illness, he was absent today.

4. Not only … but also … (Không những … mà còn …)

He is not only rich but also smart.

5. Either … or … (Hoặc … hoặc …)

He can either do his homework or play games on Sunday.

6. Neither … nor … (Không … cũng không …)

He can neither play games nor go out on Sunday. (không thêm “not”)

7. Instead of + V-ing / Noun (Thay vì)

Instead of playing games, he helped his mother.
